Tight

Double Wing  ·  18 plays  ·  11 players, 2 tight ends, 2 wings, 1 fullback

Base formation. Line splits are foot-to-foot. Ends are on the line of scrimmage. Wings align one yard outside and one yard behind their end in a two-point stance. FB is four yards deep, directly behind the QB. QB is under center.

Coaching notes

  • Foot-to-foot splits are the whole point: the defense cannot fit gaps that do not exist.
  • Both ends must be ON the line. Both wings must be OFF it. Check this every snap in practice or you will get illegal formation flags.
  • The formation is symmetric, so every play has a left version and a right version with identical rules.

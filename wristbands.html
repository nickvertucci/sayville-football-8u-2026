Wristbands

Every play a coach can call, 1 to 42, split across the three pouches of a band — one formation to a pouch. The panels are 5″ by 3″, which is a pouch, so a sheet is one whole wristband: print a copy per boy, cut on the dashed lines, and load them top to bottom.

A row is the number, then the call exactly as it is yelled, minus the formation the pouch already names: 1 · Z Right 36 Handoff is Regular I Z Right 36 Handoff.

The numbers are the ones on the cards and down the left of the call sheet, so “42” in the huddle, on the band and on the diagram are the same play. Reprint whenever a play is added: numbers are never reused, so the bands already out there stay right.

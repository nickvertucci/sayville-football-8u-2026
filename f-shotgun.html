Shotgun

6 plays  ·  11 players, 2 tight ends, quarterback, 2 halfbacks, 1 slot

Both ends are tight on the line and the slot is just outside the tight end, one step off the line, which keeps seven on the line. The quarterback takes the snap five yards deep, and the two halfbacks stand level with him, two yards either side, where they can step up and pick up the rush. The 3-back is the left halfback (odd holes) and the 2-back is the right halfback (even holes). The call names the slot's side every time — Slot Right or Slot Left.

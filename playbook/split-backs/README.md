**Formation coaching notes**

- Two backs, not three. There is no spare man in this backfield — on every snap one carries and the other one blocks or lies, so a back who coasts on a fake gives the whole play away.
- The man split out wide is the point of this formation. He blocks the corner on Pitch Right and cracks the linebacker on Counter Right — jobs a three-back backfield has to spend a back on.
- Both backs leave from the same depth and the same split, so nothing in the alignment says which one is getting it.
- The SL lines up right on twelve of the fourteen, and the call says so — SL Right. The two that put him on the left are the Keep Left and the Reverse Right, and they are the same picture: the Keep runs to the side he is on, and the Reverse is that Keep with the ball coming back the other way. Naming his side on every play from the start is why neither one cost any new call language, only the other word.
- Count seven on the line every snap. The SL creeping up onto the line is the illegal-formation flag this look draws — he is off it and split out, and he stays there.
- Everything right gets the SL's block and everything left does not — except the Keep and the Reverse, which take him with them. Run left when they start overplaying him, and know before you call it that the corner out there is not blocked.

## Split formation - Strong right - Quick pitch right

**Call it:** `Split SL Right 28 Pitch`

![Split formation - Strong right - Quick pitch right](cards/sb-pitch-r-5-3.svg)

Get outside in a hurry. The far back trails the quarterback, takes the pitch on the run and turns up inside the block out on the edge. The split man makes that block — their corner, or their outside linebacker against the 5-4-2, which has no corners — and that is what frees both backs to stay in the backfield where the fake lives.

**Coaching points**

- The pitch goes early. A quarterback who waits to be tackled first will pitch it on the ground.
- This is not a read at this age — tell him before the snap that he is pitching it.
- Everything depends on the edge being blocked, and out here the split man does it — which is what frees both backs to fake and lead. Drill him on beating that man to the spot without holding.

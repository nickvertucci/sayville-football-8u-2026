House Dive Left

House 21 DiveRUNFBvs 5-3

The fastest handoff in the book. The fullback is going before the defense has read anything, and both backs leave on sweep paths so the linebackers have three things to look at and about half a second to pick.

Assignments

LTE
Block the end over you out. He is the man who closes this down if you let him step inside.
LT
The tackle is on your inside shoulder. Drive him out toward the sideline — every step you move him is another step wide the hole gets.
LG
Nobody is over you. Go straight to the middle linebacker and get your head across him. He is the only man who can make this tackle for a short gain.
C
You have the nose, head up on you. Drive him to the backside — the hole is on the other side of your body, so he cannot cross your face.
RG
Nobody is over you. Help the center drive the nose away from the hole and stay on him.
RT
The tackle is on your inside shoulder. Block him down and seal him inside — nobody crosses your face.
RTE
Cut off pursuit from behind. Nobody chases this down from the backside.
Z
Lead through the hole a step in front of the fullback and block the first linebacker who fills it. Get your head across him.
QB
Open left and give it to the fullback now. This is a quick handoff, not a ride — he is past you before you finish turning.
FB
Two steps and you are through the hole, just off the outside hip of the guard. Pads low, ball in both arms, hit it downhill. Do not dance and do not look for anything better.
TB
Run the sweep path the other way at full speed with your arms tucked. You never get the ball on this play and you are the reason it works.

Coaching points

  • This is the first play you install in this formation and the one you fall back on all season. It works because it is fast, not because it is clever.
  • The fullback's aiming point is the outside hip of the guard, not the centre's back. If he aims at the centre he runs into the nose.
  • The far back's fake is worth more than most blocks in this book. If he jogs it, their backside linebacker is free to make the tackle. Make him sprint.
  • If the middle linebacker is meeting the fullback in the hole every time, the guard is not getting to him. Check that before you blame the back.

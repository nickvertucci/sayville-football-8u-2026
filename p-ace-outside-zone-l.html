Ace Outside Zone Left

Ace 7 ZoneRUNTBvs 5-3
All Ace plays

The same footwork stretched to the sideline. It forces their whole defense to run sideways, and if anybody trips over anybody the back cuts up behind them.

Assignments

X
Block the defender covering you and screen him away from the sideline.
LT
Reach the man on or outside you. If you cannot reach him, run him past the play.
LG
Reach the man on or outside you and get your head across.
C
Reach playside. If nobody is there, cut off the backside.
RG
Reach playside and cut off anybody chasing.
RT
Block back and cut off pursuit. You are the last one to the ball.
TE
Block back and cut off pursuit. You are the last one to the ball.
W
Block the defender covering you. Get in his way and stay there.
Z
Chase across behind the line and cut off anybody trailing the play.
QB
Open to the playside, hand it deep to the back on the run, then fake the other way.
TB
Take the ball on the run and stretch it toward the sideline. Look inside as you go — the moment a crease opens, plant and go north.

Coaching points

  • The back's eyes are inside while his feet go outside. That is the hard part — drill it walking.
  • If their end chases hard down the line, the crease is behind him. Tell the back to expect it.

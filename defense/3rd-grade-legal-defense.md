# Legal defensive formations — 3rd grade tackle (8-year-olds)

**Short answer: yes, we have a legal defense.** Our base **5-3** is legal, and so are the
other two fronts in this folder (**6-3** goal line, **4-4** wide). All three are checked
against the rulebook by the generator on every build — an illegal front fails the build.

Source: 2025 Suffolk County PAL rulebook (updated 10/5/2025), still the book for 2026.
See [`../rulebook/README.md`](../rulebook/README.md) and [`../RULES.md`](../RULES.md).

---

## First: confirm the division (this decides everything)

Rule **5.03** — 3rd grade / 8-year-olds may play **either 8-man OR 11-man** tackle.

- **11-man division** → everything in this repo is legal. Read on.
- **8-man "Rookie Tackle" division (Section 19)** → **none of this applies.** That is a
  separate rulebook: 8 players, 5 linemen and 3 backs, its own formation rules. Do not
  install any of these fronts until you know which division we're in.

**Confirm with the league before your first practice.**

## The rule that governs defense: PAL 9.02

Verbatim, in full:

> 9.02 – SPECIALIZED GAME MODIFICATIONS FOR INCREASED SAFETY
>
> NO BLITZING PERMITTED 8- & 9-YEAR OLDS IN ALL DIVISIONS.
> IN ASSOCIATION WITH THIS NEW RULE IN DIVISIONS 1 & 2, THE FOLLOWING DEFENSIVE FORMATIONS ARE REQUIRED.
> 1. A MAXIMUM OF A 6 MAN DEFENSIVE LINE. GAP PENETRATION IS ALLOWED.
> 2. A MINIMUM OF 3 LINEBACKERS AT A DISTANCE OF 2 YARDS BEHIND THE LINE OF SCRIMMAGE.
> 3. DEFENSIVE BACKS MAY COVER THE WIDE OUTS BUT MUST BE AT A MINIMUM DISTANCE OF 2 YARDS BENIND THE LINE OF SCRIMMAGE.

`BENIND` is the rulebook's spelling. The second line is what makes the scope a genuine
question — see [the open question](#one-open-question-worth-a-call-to-the-league) at the
bottom. Full text: [`../rulebook/2025-PAL-RULE-BOOK.txt`](../rulebook/2025-PAL-RULE-BOOK.txt).

Boiled down to a checklist any legal front must pass:

| # | Requirement | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Down linemen | **6 maximum** |
| 2 | Linebackers | **3 minimum** |
| 3 | Linebacker depth | **≥ 2 yards** off the ball |
| 4 | Defensive back depth | **≥ 2 yards** off the ball |
| 5 | Blitzing | **Never** — no defender moves forward before the snap |
| 6 | Gap penetration | **Allowed** (after the snap — that is not a blitz) |
| 7 | On the line (NFHS, 9.01) | offense needs 7; defense is not capped, just the 6-DL rule above |

⚠️ **The 6-2 is illegal here.** It's the most common youth front in the country, but it
has only two linebackers and fails requirement #2. Do not line up in a 6-2, and expect
that no legal opponent will show you one either.

*(Block quotes in this file are the league's words. Everything else is ours.)*

## Our fronts vs. the checklist (all verified legal)

| Front | Call | DL | LB | DB | Legal? | Why |
|---|---|----|----|----|--------|-----|
| **5-3** | Base | 5 | 3 | 3 | ✅ | 5 DL (≤6), 3 LB at 3yd (≥2), 3 DB at 3yd+ (≥2) |
| **6-3** | Goal line | 6 | 3 | 2 | ✅ | 6 DL is the cap, 3 LB and 2 corners all at 2yd — the minimum depth |
| **4-4** | Wide | 4 | 4 | 3 | ✅ | Comfortably inside every limit |
| **6-2-3** | Prevent | 6 | 2 | 3 | ✅\* | **Required** at an 18-pt lead by 15.04, which overrides the 3-LB minimum |

\* The Prevent front has only 2 linebackers, which normally fails #2. It is legal **only**
because rule 15.04 specifically mandates a 6-2-3 once you lead by 18 (see below). The
generator carries an explicit, documented exemption for this one front and nothing else.

### Recommended base for 3rd grade: the **5-3**

It's the everyday front for a reason — five down linemen cover the offensive line, three
linebackers fill, and a free safety cleans up. It satisfies 9.02 with room to spare and
gives you a last line of defense (the 6-3 goal-line front has no safety, so don't call it
outside the 5-yard line).

## Two rulebook traps that will get a coach flagged

1. **Nobody creeps forward before the snap.** A linebacker leaning in, an end timing the
   count — that reads as a blitz. Penalty is **15 yards on the head coach**, and a second
   offense is **ejection**. Teach LBs to read one step, *then* attack downhill.

2. **The 18-point rule is mandatory, not a choice (15.04–15.09).** The moment you lead by
   18 (after the extra point) you **must** switch to the 6-2-3 Prevent: two LBs 5 yards
   off, three safeties 5 yards behind them, ends 7 yards outside the tackles — and you
   must also **replace your starting backfield, including quarterback**, on offense
   (15.03). On a roster of 15 or more those players *leave the game* on offense; only a
   roster of 14 or less may keep them in, and then only on the offensive line tackle to
   tackle, never carrying the ball. Ours starts the season at 17 to 32 (7.01), so they
   come off. Know this cold *before* it happens on the field. It's in the book as the
   **Prevent** front.

## One open question worth a call to the league

Rule 9.02 says no-blitz applies to **"all divisions,"** but the sentence introducing the
*formation* requirements (6-DL cap, 3-LB minimum) is qualified **"in Divisions 1 & 2."**
Divisions here are competitive tiers from last year's record (14.02), not age groups — so
it's genuinely unclear whether the formation caps bind a Division 3+ team.

**This playbook assumes they bind us**, because complying costs nothing and guessing wrong
costs 15 yards and a possible ejection. Worth one email to the league to confirm.

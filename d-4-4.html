4-4

Base4 down4 LB3 DB

Four down linemen on the guards and the ends. Two inside linebackers and two outside linebackers at three yards, with the outside pair responsible for forcing everything back in. Two corners and a free safety behind. This is the front we play unless there is a reason not to.

Assignments

LDE
Attack the outside shoulder of the man over you and keep your outside arm free. Squeeze down, but never let anything cross your face to the outside.
LDG
Attack the inside shoulder of the guard and squeeze the middle. You and the other guard keep the center from climbing to a linebacker.
RDG
Attack the inside shoulder of the guard and squeeze the middle. You and the other guard keep the center from climbing to a linebacker.
RDE
Attack the outside shoulder of the man over you and keep your outside arm free. Squeeze down, but never let anything cross your face to the outside.
LOLB
Outside linebacker. You are the force man — turn everything back inside and never get hooked. If it is a pass, you have the flat.
LILB
Three yards deep. Read the back. Fill the first open gap inside and meet the blocker in the hole, not behind it.
RILB
Three yards deep. Read the back. Fill the first open gap inside and meet the blocker in the hole, not behind it.
ROLB
Outside linebacker. You are the force man — turn everything back inside and never get hooked. If it is a pass, you have the flat.
LC
Three yards off, outside the end. Nothing outside you and nothing behind you. Force it back in.
RC
Three yards off, outside the end. Nothing outside you and nothing behind you. Force it back in.
FS
Eight yards deep in the middle. Nobody gets behind you. Take the cut-off angle, not the chase angle.

Coaching points

  • This is our base, so it is the front the kids should be able to line up in without being told. Nearly everything that hurts you at this age gets outside, and two extra defenders who can run beats two extra defenders who can only push.
  • The two outside linebackers are the reason this front works. They force everything back inside — if either one gets hooked and the ball gets outside him, it is a long run every time.
  • We give up a body inside to get it, so an offense that runs straight at the middle will find room. If they start hammering the A gaps, call Tight and put the fifth man back on the line.
  • The two inside linebackers are stacked behind the guards, not over the center, so neither one owns the middle by himself. Whichever one the ball declares to fills it, and the other takes the cutback — say it every practice or they both chase.
  • Nobody crosses the line before the snap. The linebackers read one step, then go.

Why it is legal

Four down linemen, four linebackers at three yards, and three defensive backs no closer than three yards. Comfortably inside every limit. No blitzing.

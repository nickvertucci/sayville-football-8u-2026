4-4

Wide4 down4 LB3 DB

Four down linemen on the guards and the ends. Two inside linebackers and two outside linebackers at three yards, with the outside pair responsible for forcing everything back in. Two corners and a free safety behind.

Assignments

LE
Attack the outside shoulder of the man over you and keep your outside arm free. Squeeze down, but never let anything cross your face to the outside.
LT
Attack the inside shoulder of the guard and squeeze the middle. You and the other tackle keep the centre from climbing to a linebacker.
RT
Attack the inside shoulder of the guard and squeeze the middle. You and the other tackle keep the centre from climbing to a linebacker.
RE
Attack the outside shoulder of the man over you and keep your outside arm free. Squeeze down, but never let anything cross your face to the outside.
W
Outside linebacker. You are the force man — turn everything back inside and never get hooked. If it is a pass, you have the flat.
M
Three yards deep. Read the back. Fill the first open gap inside and meet the blocker in the hole, not behind it.

Outside linebacker. You are the force man — turn everything back inside and never get hooked. If it is a pass, you have the flat.
LC
Three yards off, outside the end. Nothing outside you and nothing behind you. Force it back in.
RC
Three yards off, outside the end. Nothing outside you and nothing behind you. Force it back in.
FS
Eight yards deep in the middle. Nobody gets behind you. Take the cut-off angle, not the chase angle.

Coaching points

  • Call this when they keep getting outside us. Two extra defenders who can run beats two extra defenders who can only push.
  • The two outside linebackers are the reason this front works. They force everything back inside — if either one gets hooked and the ball gets outside him, it is a long run every time.
  • We give up a body inside to get it, so an offence that runs straight at the middle will find room. If they start hammering the A gaps, go back to the 5-3.
  • Nobody crosses the line before the snap. The linebackers read one step, then go.

Why it is legal

Four down linemen, four linebackers at three yards, and three defensive backs no closer than three yards. Comfortably inside every limit. No blitzing.

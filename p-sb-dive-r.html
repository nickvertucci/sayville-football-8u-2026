Split Dive Right

Split Z Right 30 DiveRUNRHvs 5-3

The near back straight ahead off a double team on the nose, going right through the first gap outside the center. This is the play that has to work before anything else in the formation does — it is what holds their linebackers inside and makes the pitch go.

Assignments

LTE
Cut off pursuit from behind. You are the last one to the ball, so never quit on the play.
LT
The tackle is on your inside shoulder. Get your head across him and wall him off so he cannot chase.
LG
Nobody is over you. Step back inside and cut off anyone chasing through the middle.
C
You and the playside guard double the nose. Drive him off the spot — the hole is right off his back.
RG
Nobody is over you. Double the nose with the center, and come off onto the middle linebacker when he shows.
RT
The tackle is on your inside shoulder. Block him down and seal him inside — that is what opens the hole.
RTE
The end is head up on you. Drive him out and away from the ball.
Z
Sprint at the corner over you and stay in front of him. If this one breaks the line he is the last man between it and the goal line.
QB
Take the snap, open right and hand the ball to the near back at two yards. Then carry out the pitch fake the other way.
LH
Run the pitch path away at full speed with your arms tucked. You are the lie.
RH
Aim at the outside hip of the center, right off the double team. Take the ball and get north — there is no hole to look for.

Coaching points

  • Snap to handoff should be under a second. If the back is waiting on the ball, the play is already dead.
  • The back away from the ball runs his pitch path every single time. Two backs means the fake is half the play, not a spare part of it.
  • The ball carrier runs at a spot, not at a defender. Give him the outside hip of the center and let him go.
  • Run it both ways. Once their middle linebacker knows which gap the dive hits, it stops being quick.

Ace

Single Back  ·  3 plays  ·  11 players, split end, tight end, slot, flanker, quarterback, tailback

One back, four receivers spread across the field. This is our spread-them-out formation: it pulls defenders away from the middle and gives us the widest, thinnest box we will see all game. Seven on the line — split end, five interior linemen and the tight end — with the slot and the flanker off it.

Coaching notes

  • Only run this if the kids can line up correctly without being walked out there. Four receivers is four chances to get an illegal formation flag.
  • One back means we cannot out-muscle anybody. Everything here works by making them defend width first.
  • The split end is ON the line and the slot is OFF it. Check it every snap.
  • Use this late in a game when they have stacked eight men in the box against our other formations.

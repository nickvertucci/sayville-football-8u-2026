How we call plays

Every play has a teaching name (Power Right) and a huddle call (Tight 6 Power). Both are printed on every card. The call is always formation + hole + play word, and the hole numbers are the only thing the kids have to memorize — odd to the left, even to the right, counting outward.

0
Straight up the middle
1 / 2
A gap — left / right of the center
3 / 4
B gap — between guard and tackle
5 / 6
Off tackle — outside the tackle
7 / 8
Outside — around the end

So Bone 8 Pitch is the wishbone, outside right, pitch, and I 4 Iso is the I-formation, right B gap, isolation — a kid who knows the system can run either one the first time he hears it.

Before you install anything

The league rulebook constrains this playbook in ways that matter: 8- and 9-year-olds face a minimum of three linebackers and no blitzing, which makes the 6-2 illegal, and 8-year-olds may be placed in an 8-man division where none of this is legal at all.

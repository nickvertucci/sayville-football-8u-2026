I Wishbone

I

I-Formation  ·  8 plays  ·  11 players, 2 tight ends, 1 flanker, quarterback, fullback, tailback

Normal line splits — about one foot, not the foot-to-foot splits we use in Tight. Both ends are ON the line. The flanker is OFF the line, one yard outside the end and one yard back, so we always have exactly seven on the line. Fullback at three yards, tailback stacked directly behind him at five and a half.

Coaching notes

  • This formation exists so the tailback can pick a hole. In Tight the play decides where the ball goes; here the back reads the block in front of him. That is a harder skill — do not install it until the double wing runs itself.
  • The flanker is always to the right. That makes us strong right, which is fine — we would rather have one look the kids never line up wrong than two they do.
  • Count seven on the line every single snap. The flanker creeping up to the line is the most common illegal formation flag you will get out of this look.
  • Fullback and tailback must be stacked. If the tailback drifts he tips which way the play is going before the snap.

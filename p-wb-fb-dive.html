Bone Dive

Bone 2 DiveRUNFBvs 5-3

The fullback straight up the middle, right now. This is the play that has to work before anything else in the formation does — it is what holds their linebackers inside.

Assignments

LE
The end is head up on you. Drive him out and away from the ball.
LT
Climb straight to the linebacker on your side. The tackle inside you belongs to the guard.
LG
Nobody is over you. Block out on the tackle in the gap outside you and open the hole.

You have the nose alone, head up. Drive him straight back off the ball. Everything on this play depends on it.

Coaching points

  • Both halfbacks run their fakes every single time. Three threats or none.
  • The fullback does not read anything. He runs at the center's back and keeps his feet moving.

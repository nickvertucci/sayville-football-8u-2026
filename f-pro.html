Tight I Wing-T Pro Wishbone Ace

Pro

Pro Set  ·  5 plays  ·  11 players, split end, tight end, flanker, quarterback, halfback, fullback

Two backs split behind the quarterback, a tight end to the right, a split end wide left and a flanker outside the tight end. This is the formation that makes a defense cover the whole field instead of stacking the middle. Seven on the line: the split end, five interior linemen and the tight end.

Coaching notes

  • The split end and the flanker may never block anybody all game. That is fine — they are out there to make two defenders leave the box.
  • Both backs are the same depth, so neither one tips the play. Do not let them cheat forward on their running plays.
  • Count seven on the line every snap. The flanker must be off it and the split end must be on it.

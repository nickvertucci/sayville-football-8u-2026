Regular I SL Right 33 IsoRUNTB

The same isolation block the other way. The SL stays where he is, so this runs away from our strength — which is exactly why it is open.

Blocked against

Assignments vs 4-4

LTE
End on your inside shoulder. Drive him to the sideline. The ball goes inside you.
LT
Block down on the tackle, inside shoulder. Head across him — nobody crosses your face.
LG
Tackle head up on you. Hands inside, pads under his, drive him back.
C
Nobody on you. Climb to the playside linebacker. Head across him.
RG
Tackle head up on you. Cut him off — get between him and the ball.
RT
Nobody on you. Cut off the backside. Never quit on the play.
RTE
End on your inside shoulder. Cut him off — get between him and the ball.
SL
Cut off the backside. Never quit on the play.
QB
Reverse pivot, hand to the tailback at four yards, then clear out and fake wide.
FB
Lead through the hole. Block the first man who shows in it. Move him any direction — just move him.
TB
Take the handoff at four yards, get downhill behind the fullback, and cut off his block.

Coaching points

  • Running away from the SL means one fewer blocker out there, so the tailback has to get north fast.
  • Their defense will usually shade toward our SL. That is the reason this play exists — check it before you call it.
  • Same block, same read as I Iso Right. Do not teach it as a new play, teach it as the same play flipped.

Trips

8 plays  ·  11 players, 2 tight ends, quarterback, fullback, tailback, slot — the fullback behind the quarterback, the rest of the strength out wide

Seven on the line every snap: the five up front, the X tight on the left end, and the Y stretched out on the right end — wide, but down in his stance. The tailback and the Z are the two off the line beside him, the tailback inside and the Z outside. The fullback is the only man in the backfield, behind the quarterback, and he is the lead blocker on everything that goes around the end. The call names the side — Trips Right or Trips Left — and the whole strong side moves with it, the stretched end included.

Coaching notes

  • Count seven on the line every snap. The Y is stretched a long way out but he is ON the line and down in his stance — standing him up is an illegal-formation flag, and it is the easiest one to give away in this look.
  • The tailback and the Z are OFF the line, a yard back, one either side of the Y: tailback inside, Z outside. Either of them creeping up is the same flag from the other direction.
  • The fullback is behind the quarterback and he is the only back there. On a sweep he bubbles out around the end and blocks the first man outside — he does not run up into the line.
  • Trips Right puts the stretch on the right and the X tight on the left; Trips Left is the mirror, and it is the X who stretches out. Whichever end is tight is the one who comes across on the end-around.
  • Trips does not run inside — no Power, no Smash, no Dive. Three men to a side is an edge look, and everything off it goes outside or into the air.

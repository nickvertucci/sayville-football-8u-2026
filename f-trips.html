Trips

8 plays  ·  11 players, 2 tight ends, quarterback, fullback, tailback, slot — no one behind the quarterback

Same line as the Regular I — both ends tight, seven on the line every snap. Nobody behind the quarterback. The bunch is a triangle: the slot just outside the tight end and the fullback outside him, both up front, with the tailback a yard back splitting the two of them. The call names the bunch — Trips Right or Trips Left — and moves all three.

Coaching notes

  • Count seven on the line every snap — both ends stay down. 2, 3 and 4 are off the line; any of them creeping up is an illegal-formation flag.
  • Nobody in the backfield. The quarterback is alone. The bunch is a triangle: slot inside and fullback outside up front, tailback behind, splitting them.
  • The tailback lines up in the gap between the slot and the fullback, one yard back. He should be able to see daylight between their inside shoulders.
  • Trips Right is all three on the right; Trips Left moves all three. A play that moves them says so in the call.
  • Same numbers as the Regular I: 2 is the fullback, 3 the tailback, 4 the slot. Trips does not run inside — no Power, no Smash, no Dive. Three men to a side is an edge look, and everything off it goes outside or into the air.
